What we built this weekend, all on testnet: the open registry and search, RLUSD x402 plus XRP-to-RLUSD through the AMM, verified sellers, the Mastercard AP4M rail, a dedicated QuickNode endpoint, and Ledger-held merchant payouts with clear signing on Speculos. Next: mainnet RLUSD, a physical Ledger Nano, more verticals, and a payout dashboard. Later: procurement agents with company spend policies, hardware signing on the buyer side, and publishing the protocol as an open spec.
