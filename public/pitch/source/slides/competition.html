Placement is our reading of each product’s public announcements.

Where we sit. On one axis, reach: is your catalog only reachable through a couple of chat apps, or by any HTTP agent? On the other, settlement: card rails that take days, or on-ledger in seconds. OpenAI Instant Checkout with Stripe’s ACP and Shopify’s agentic checkout are card-based and tied to specific surfaces. Google AP2 and Visa Intelligent Commerce are more open protocols, but still card-settled. Coinbase’s x402 Bazaar has crypto payment rails, but no merchant onboarding, catalog or verified-seller layer. Borneo is the open, merchant-first layer that settles on XRPL in seconds. Mastercard AP4M isn’t a competitor here: it’s one of our rails.
