Sponsor stack

Where each sponsor sits

Ripple · XRPL

Settlement and trust. RLUSD, x402, AMM payments, XLS-70 credentials, XLS-40 DIDs.

Mastercard

A buyer rail. AP4M channels: open once with a cap, buy, redeem a voucher per purchase.

QuickNode

The pipe to the ledger. Dedicated XRPL RPC and WebSocket for every settle, AMM quote, credential check and TrustSet.

Ledger

The merchant’s keys. Payout address held in hardware, with clear signing for TrustSet.

A quick map of the sponsors. XRPL is settlement and trust. Mastercard AP4M is one of the buyer’s payment rails. QuickNode is our dedicated endpoint to the ledger: every x402 settle, every AMM path, every credential check and every Ledger-signed TrustSet goes through it, so checkout doesn’t depend on rate-limited public nodes. Ledger holds the merchant’s payout keys.
