Here’s the gap in numbers. 42% of consumers already use AI tools to shop, according to NielsenIQ. But only 11% of small merchants are ready to sell to AI agents, according to the PYMNTS and Visa shopping index. Put those side by side and, roughly, for every one buyer there’s about a quarter of a seller. Demand isn’t the bottleneck. Supply is. If asked: these are two different surveys, so the ratio is approximate, not one measured number.
