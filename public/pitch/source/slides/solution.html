The solution

Talk. Publish. Get paid.

STEP 1

Talk

Tell the setup agent what you sell, or drop a CSV or your store URL.

STEP 2

Publish

You go live on an open registry. Any HTTP agent can find you.

STEP 3

Get paid

Buyers pay in RLUSD, XRP or Mastercard. You always get RLUSD.

About 5 minutes. No code, no dashboard, no new checkout to build.

Borneo fixes that in three steps. Talk: the merchant chats with our setup agent, or drops a CSV, or pastes their store URL. Publish: they’re listed on an open registry with llms.txt and a search API, so any HTTP agent can discover them, not just two chat apps. Get paid: buyers pay however they like, RLUSD, XRP or a Mastercard agent payment, and the merchant always receives RLUSD on the XRP Ledger. About five minutes, no code.
