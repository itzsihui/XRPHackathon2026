How it’s wired. Every part of Borneo that touches the XRP Ledger, the buyer agent’s x402 and XRP settlement, FX quotes through the AMM, seller verification, and the Ledger TrustSet on merchant setup, goes through one dedicated QuickNode endpoint, over HTTPS JSON-RPC for reads and WebSocket for submits. Three reasons: it’s not shared, so there are no public-node rate limits in the middle of a checkout; WebSocket submit-and-wait lets the agent tell the buyer the payment validated inside the same chat turn; and it’s configuration, not code: two env vars, so moving to mainnet is a swap, not a rewrite.
