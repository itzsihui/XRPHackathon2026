Three ways to pay, one way to get paid

Agent answers the HTTP 402 challenge and pays the exact RLUSD quote on XRPL.

XRP, auto-converted

One cross-currency Payment through the XRPL AMM: buyer spends XRP, merchant receives RLUSD.

MERCHANT RECEIVES

RLUSD

Like a tourist card terminal: the buyer picks the currency, the merchant’s books stay in one.

Checkout is multi-rail. The buyer’s agent can pay in RLUSD through x402, in XRP that the ledger converts through the AMM in one Payment, or through Mastercard Agent Pay, AP4M, where the agent opens a capped payment channel and each purchase redeems a voucher. Whatever the buyer uses, the merchant ends up with RLUSD. Mastercard is one rail among three: it brings card-network trust and spending caps to agents without the merchant integrating anything new.
