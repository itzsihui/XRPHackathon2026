Checkout is only as reliable as the node

One agent purchase hits the XRP Ledger several times. If any call stalls, the agent’s checkout stalls with it.

Why QuickNode matters to us. One agent purchase isn’t one call: we verify the seller’s credential and DID, check the payout address has an RLUSD trust line, quote the XRP-to-RLUSD path through amm_info and ripple_path_find, then submit the payment and wait for validation. Merchant onboarding submits the Ledger-signed TrustSet the same way. Every one of these goes through our dedicated QuickNode endpoint, reads over JSON-RPC and everything that needs a live connection over WebSocket. If any of those calls stalls on a shared public node, the agent’s checkout stalls in front of the buyer.
