Give agents a budget, not a card number

Capped

The buyer’s agent opens one payment channel with a hard RLUSD cap. It can never spend past it.

Accountable

The agent signs in with a registered wallet and pays a known merchant ID. Every spend is attributable.

Instant

Each purchase redeems a voucher in RLUSD on XRPL EVM. No per-purchase card authorization round trip.

For Borneo merchants: Mastercard-backed agent payments, zero extra integration.

Same locked quote as every other rail. The merchant doesn’t change a thing.

Why Mastercard Agent Pay matters to us. Handing an agent a raw card number is the wrong model: it’s unlimited, hard to attribute, and every purchase needs a full card authorization. AP4M gives the agent a budget instead. The agent opens one payment channel with a hard cap in RLUSD; it signs in with a registered wallet and pays a known merchant ID, so every spend is attributable; and each purchase is just a voucher redemption on XRPL EVM, so it’s instant. For Borneo merchants, that means they accept Mastercard-backed agent payments without integrating anything: the same locked quote goes down the AP4M rail.
