The problem · merchant side

Agents can’t shop your store

Your customers are becoming agents. Your store was built for humans.
Picture a small merchant, say a Singapore T-shirt brand with a normal web store. More and more of their customers are not people anymore, they’re agents: procurement bots, personal assistants, local LLMs, coding agents. Watch what happens. They hit CAPTCHAs, login walls, card forms, 3DS, and pages that change layout every week, and they bounce. The only agents that get through are the two big chat apps, through a gated partner catalog. So the merchant is effectively invisible to the long tail of agents that want to buy.
