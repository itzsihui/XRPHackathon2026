The potential. Juniper Research forecasts agentic commerce growing from about 8 billion dollars in 2026 to 1.5 trillion by 2030. Our wedge is small cross-border merchants in Southeast Asia, starting with fashion, where card fees and payout delays hurt most. Then B2B procurement agents, then any vertical with a catalog. We earn three ways: a 1% take rate on settled volume, about a third of typical card processing, a 99 dollar annual fee for the verified-seller credential, and promoted placement in registry search.
