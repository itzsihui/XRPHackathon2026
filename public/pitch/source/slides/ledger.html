For the Ledger track: the merchant’s payout address lives on a Ledger, not as a seed in an env file. Four steps: connect and derive the r-address on the device, fund the XRP reserve from the faucet, enable RLUSD by signing a TrustSet on the device, and bind that address as the store’s payTo. Ledger Wallet today only shows send and receive, so we built a clear-signing preview for TrustSet: the merchant reads in plain English “allow this store to receive RLUSD, limit one million” before approving. Built with the Device Management Kit and XRP signer kit, running on Speculos for the demo.
